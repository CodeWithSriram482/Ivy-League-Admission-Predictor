# Ivy-League-Admission-Predictor
## **Problem Statement**  

Jamboree, a leading educational institution, has a strong track record of helping students secure admissions to top global universities through effective test preparation strategies for GMAT, GRE, and SAT. To further assist students, Jamboree has introduced an admission probability assessment feature on its website, specifically designed for Indian applicants targeting Ivy League colleges.  

To enhance the accuracy of this tool, we aim to analyze key factors influencing graduate admissions and their interdependencies. By applying **Linear Regression**, we will develop a predictive model to estimate an individual’s chance of admission based on various parameters. This data-driven approach will enable Jamboree to refine its admission prediction system, offering students a precise and informed evaluation of their admission prospects at top universities.
